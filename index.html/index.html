My Story

From curious beginner to trusted design partner

I started out fascinated by how a few lines of code and a well-placed pixel could completely change how people feel about a brand. That curiosity turned into a career.

Today, I work with startup founders, agencies and growing businesses to design and build websites that don't just look premium — they're built to convert visitors into leads, customers and bookings.

My toolkit blends modern no-code platforms like Framer and Webflow with strong UI/UX fundamentals from Figma, so every project is fast to launch, easy to maintain, and genuinely beautiful.

Whether you need a complete brand-new site, a Figma-to-Framer conversion, or a redesign that finally feels "you" — I'm here to make it happen, end to end.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does a typical project take?+

Most landing pages take 3–7 days, while full websites typically take 1–3 weeks depending on scope, number of pages, and how quickly content and feedback are provided.

How much does a website cost?+

Projects generally start from $1,000 for a single landing page and range up to $10,000+ for larger multi-page Framer builds with custom interactions. I'll give you a fixed quote after our discovery call.

How many revisions are included?+

Every project includes two rounds of revisions as standard. Additional revision rounds can be added if needed — we'll agree on this upfront so there are no surprises.

Do you build in Framer specifically?+

Yes — Framer is one of my core platforms. I design directly in Framer or convert existing Figma designs into fully responsive, animated Framer sites with CMS support where needed.

Do you offer website maintenance after launch?+

Yes. I offer ongoing maintenance packages covering content updates, small design tweaks, performance checks and uptime monitoring so your site keeps performing after launch.

How do we communicate during the project?+

Communication happens via email or your preferred platform (Upwork, Slack, WhatsApp). I share regular progress updates and preview links so you can follow along and give feedback at every stage.
